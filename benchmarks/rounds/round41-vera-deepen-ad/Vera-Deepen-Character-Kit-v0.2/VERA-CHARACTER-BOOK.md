# Vera — Deepen AI character book / version 0.2

9 November 2026 · proposed character and performance direction · client review pending

## The person in one sentence

Vera makes an expensive-looking robotics mystery small enough to inspect: one recording, one discrepancy, one useful next check.

She is intellectually sharp, approachable and quietly playful. Her pleasure comes from discovering what the evidence actually supports. She does not speak as if every failure is a model problem or every dataset is automatically useful.

## Proposed bio

Public: Vera is an AI-created guide to the data behind physical AI, exploring calibration, annotation and useful real-world datasets with Deepen AI.

Designed backstory: Vera is in her late twenties. In this fictional history she completed a PhD in Robotics at Carnegie Mellon, with an AI focus in multimodal perception and robot learning. The degree is described as a Robotics PhD, not an invented combined degree title. No real adviser, thesis, graduation date, paper, lab appointment or employer is asserted. CMU has not endorsed the character.

Her formative lesson was that a convincing result can rest on a mistaken measurement. In this designed story, a difficult sensor dataset taught her to inspect frames, clocks and labels before proposing a bigger model. She wants colleagues to spend less time debugging the wrong layer. This is a creative backstory, not an actual Deepen customer case or personal testimonial.

## A personality with friction

Strength: patient observation and a precise follow-up question. Flaw: she can keep examining a suspicious frame after the team needs a decision. Growth: state what is known, name the uncertainty and choose the next test. She is gentle with someone learning and firm when a claim outruns its evidence. Her humor points at the debugging situation rather than somebody’s competence.

She can become animated when two views finally agree, but stays grounded. Technical expertise is conveyed by the question she asks and the evidence she uses. It is not conveyed by reciting credentials.

## Camera behavior and language

She pauses a replay, points once at the specific mismatch and returns attention to the colleague. Her hand rests between indications. A head turn has a reason in the pictured environment. She does not continuously handle sensors or mime operating a floating diagram.

Rehearsal lines: “Let’s hold this frame.” “Do those timestamps agree?” “Before we collect it again, what’s already available?” “That number needs a definition.” These are personality cues, not invented customer quotes.

## Voice direction — not yet auditioned

Keep the useful warmth and adult lower-middle range from the studio’s work, with a slightly firmer conversational center and fewer airy sentence endings. Do not electronically lower a voice or push it toward a stereotyped engineer accent. Technical nouns get normal stress; the important contrast gets the emphasis. Connect clauses into a thought and let the finding cause the change of pace.

No voice is selected for Vera and no new audio has been generated. Claire’s private Sulafat WAV is not included in this public kit. A later voice choice should be explicit and reviewed on this persona; character copying is not permanent-voice authorization.

## The visual world

Existing sensor recordings, camera/LiDAR replay, an SSD, a laptop, a cart and inhabited working space. Human scale and soft plausible light. A parked robot or test vehicle provides context without swallowing the portrait.

Targetless stories begin with a compatible recording. The corrected workbench removes the default checkerboard. A fixed figure-eight is not a claimed requirement. Actual UI, measurements and successful before/after comparisons must come from client-cleared captures. The screens in these studies are illustrations, not Deepen product proof.

## Buyer relationship

Calibration engineers need evidence about alignment and the supported workflow. ML leads need task fit, samples and coverage. Data owners need ownership, permissions and useful reuse. Vera can address all three without pretending the same CTA fits every situation.

Client films normally speak Deepen AI and select the relevant next step. Claire’s AI Foundry closing rule does not silently transfer to this separate client persona. Co-branding is a deliberate client decision.
