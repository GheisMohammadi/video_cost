## Corrections from current primary pages

The targetless page explicitly says no prescribed drive. Do not turn a figure-eight example into a requirement. The current $150 launch offer is LiDAR–camera extrinsics; other sensor configurations and parameter types run through engagements. The page describes its numeric consistency figures as repeatability rather than a blanket absolute-accuracy guarantee.

Refinery describes sample/format/rights review and different buyer/seller/partner paths. Supplier eligibility, competitor controls, anonymity, payout mechanics and a particular catalog total should be confirmed for the actual campaign rather than inherited from a general research summary.

These details shape Vera’s personality: she distinguishes a useful observation from a broader promise. Small distribution samples do not establish a winning creative formula. Test the visible failure, replay and next step as hypotheses.

## How Vera participates in the ten concepts

| Concept | Vera’s job | Evidence / adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Calibration offset | Pause and compare the same object. | Use a cleared real overlay; measured offsets need the actual report. |
