The current studio kit describes a continuous native Customer Story, up to 24 seconds, typically four lines and at most 52 spoken words. Recheck that contract before submitting. 720×1280 is the 9:16 delivery target; measure the source and deliver constant 30 fps without changing speech speed. Do not invent a native fps control if the tool does not expose one.

Vera’s meaningful action is a replay pause or one precise indication. Keep eye contact shifts motivated. Generate the voice and mouth performance together where supported, then review the actual result. A voice sample is guidance, not proof of identity, expression or synchronization.
